## Manta 0.4.11 – Trash in iCloud Drive, update window polish

- **Trash in iCloud Drive:** items in iCloud Drive can now be moved to the Trash. Where macOS refuses Manta although Finder may move them, Finder now takes over; macOS asks once whether Manta may control Finder. **⌘Z** brings the items back as usual
- **Centred buttons:** on the "Couldn't check for updates" screen, **Close** and **Try Again** now sit centred under the message, like the icon and text above them

Thanks to Lennart Novak for both reports!
