## Manta 0.4.8 – Undo and Redo

- **Undo (⌘Z) and Redo (⇧⌘Z) like in Finder:** items moved to the Trash go back where they were, a rename gets its old name back (on servers too), and a new folder or file goes away again. Several steps, in the Edit menu and the command palette. Manta never replaces anything along the way; in text fields and the terminal ⌘Z works as usual. Thanks to Lennart Novak!
