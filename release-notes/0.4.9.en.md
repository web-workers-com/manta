## Manta 0.4.9 – Applications like in Finder

- **Applications shows every app:** like in Finder, it now also lists Apple's apps (Calculator, Mail, App Store …), and Utilities also Terminal and Activity Monitor. Safari shows normally again instead of dimmed. macOS protects these apps: you can open and copy them, and when you try to rename, move or delete one, Manta tells you why it can't
- **Folders stay on top:** apps and other packages sort like files, so Utilities is back at the top
- **No more .DS_Store:** Manta never shows it, not even with hidden files on, just like Finder

Thanks to Lennart Novak for the reports!
